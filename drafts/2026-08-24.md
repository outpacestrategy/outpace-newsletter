PREVIEW TEXT:
AI search and Google's latest update are punishing the same weak websites.

---

Google's latest update didn't create weak websites. It exposed them.

I've been watching two things collide this week. Search Engine Journal reported that AI Overviews now show up in 47% of informational queries. They also found branded clicks can rise 12% when a brand gets featured. Then the August SEO update chatter got loud, with site owners reporting sharp ranking swings and weaker content losing ground.

Those aren't separate stories. They're the same story.

**The sites winning in AI search are usually the same ones already doing the basics well.**

If your site has thin service pages, weak proof, vague headlines, and no real structure, AI visibility won't save you. Google is still rewarding clarity and trust. AI tools are mostly pulling from sources that already earned that trust.

What I'd do this week is simple. Tighten your core service pages. Answer buyer questions fast. Put reviews and proof near the CTA. Clean up your internal links. Add schema where it actually helps Google understand the page.

---

- **[Local SEO]** GBP reviews, photos, and FAQs still matter more than another generic blog post. Local buyers want proof first, and Google still reads those trust signals clearly.
- **[Conversion]** Specific buttons like “Get My Free Quote” still beat vague CTAs like “Submit.” If AI search steals clicks, every visitor has to be more likely to convert.
- **[AI Content]** Generic AI copy is getting easier to spot. When your site sounds padded or fake, people bounce before they ever trust you.

---

This is exactly why we build sites around conversion and clarity first. A pretty site that doesn't build trust fast is dead weight now.

If someone landed on your site tonight, would they trust you in 10 seconds?
